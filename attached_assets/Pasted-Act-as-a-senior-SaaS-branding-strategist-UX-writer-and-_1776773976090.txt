PRODUCT:

Name: Schedora

Schedora is a structured content and outreach workflow platform.

It helps users:
- Draft and organize content using AI assistance
- Schedule posts across platforms
- Manage job posts and outreach workflows
- Control usage through a token-based system

- Keep messaging strong but credible
